## Reproduce

Use a data checkout at the SHA above, then from `analysis/`:

```sh
uv run python -m perfgate_analysis.refresh ../../perfgate-data/raw --out reports/2026-10-18
```

The report validates records against the JSON Schema, filters the protocol and app
epochs, and writes CSV tables plus a snapshot manifest. Known failed requests are
excluded alongside failed audits. `--sections rq1,rq2,rq3,power,history` can select
individual stages. Compact result tables are committed under `results/2026-10-18/`;
full per-load data remain on the `data` branch.

## Detection of measured regressions

Ten measured loads per variant, `abab`, `devtools`, Mann–Whitney plus >1% relative
effect, judged on the target metric. These are per-metric detections, before any
multiple-testing correction. Sizes in the first column are the injector's nominal
calibration; the next column is the effect actually measured.

| Target | Nominal size | Median effect | Detected | Jobs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FCP | 2% | 2.13% | 98.6% (68/69) | 69 |
| FCP | 5% | 5.14% | 100% | 69 |
| FCP | 10% | 10.15% | 100% | 69 |
| FCP | 25% | 25.26% | 100% | 69 |
| TBT | 2% | 1.43% | 39.1% | 69 |
| TBT | 5% | 4.37% | 97.1% | 69 |
| TBT | 10% | 9.27% | 94.2% | 69 |
| TBT | 25% | 24.17% | 98.6% | 69 |
| LCP | 2% | 3.64% | 100% | 67 |

The 5% median budget detects none of the 2% FCP cases and 71.0% of the nominal 5%
FCP cases. On the nominal 5% TBT case it detects 33.3%. A threshold at the effect
being tested is an unstable boundary even when the change is real. Wilson intervals
are included in the exported power table; 100% observed detection is not a guarantee.

CLS uses an **absolute** margin. With Mann–Whitney and a difference >0.005, twenty
loads detect 95.6–95.7% of nominal 5–25% changes, with one false alarm among 109 A/A
jobs (0.92%). The nominal 2% shift is about 0.003 CLS, below that margin, and is
usually missed intentionally. Five loads are insufficient on this two-valued metric.

## A verdict over several metrics and pages

For continuity with RQ3, these are **two-sided** A/A tests and an absolute relative
effect >1% on FCP/LCP/SI/TBT. They evaluate any detected difference, including apparent
improvements. They are not measurements of the proposed one-sided production gate
with a 5% TBT margin. Twenty loads per variant:

| Scope / mode | Jobs or complete suites | Uncorrected | Holm | Holm + 1% margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| One page, devtools, abab | 408 | 8.33% | 1.72% | 0.25% |
| One page, devtools, sequential | 407 | 31.20% | 19.16% | 6.39% |
| Four pages, devtools, abab | 94 | 27.66% | 3.19% | 0% |
| Four pages, devtools, sequential | 93 | 76.34% | 36.56% | 20.43% |
| Four pages, simulate, abab | 94 | 31.91% | 1.06% | 1.06% |
| Four pages, simulate, sequential | 94 | 51.06% | 11.70% | 9.57% |

Four-page suites group the four jobs from one workflow run and attempt, with a
single Holm correction over all 14 page × metric hypotheses. Each page ran on its
own runner, as in a matrix CI check. Incomplete suites are excluded. Zero alarms in
94 suites has a non-zero upper confidence bound; intervals are in the CSV tables.
BH and results at 5/10 loads are exported too. Holm remains the conservative choice
for a decision to block a PR; BH is not a general family-wise error guarantee.

## Analysis corrections

- Bootstrap rules have a 200-replicate computational cap. Capped draws are now
  selected reproducibly from the **entire** pool, rather than its earliest jobs.
  Uncapped rules use the full pool; the effective replicate counts are explicit.
- History adjustment is trained strictly before each evaluated window. A guarded
  variant applies the benchmark slope only with at least 12 past jobs and p<0.05.
  Missing benchmark scores fall back to no adjustment for that job.
- History variants use identical permutations for each window and injected effect.
  The zero-effect step and ramp therefore have identical false-alarm estimates.
- Both 20- and 40-job windows are evaluated. Windows overlap, and permutation tests
  assume exchangeability; the results are empirical diagnostics, not a guarantee of
  a calibrated online alarm rate on temporally dependent history.

## History: sensitivity and the cost of longer windows

Heavy page, `devtools`, E-Divisive with past-only, significance-guarded benchmark
adjustment. MDE means detection in at least 80% of the evaluated windows:

| Window | Metric | Step MDE | Ramp MDE | A/A windows flagged |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 20 jobs | FCP | 1% | 2% | 1.27% (1/79) |
| 20 jobs | LCP | 5% | 5% | 5.06% (4/79) |
| 20 jobs | TBT | 10% | 15% | 3.80% (3/79) |
| 40 jobs | FCP | 1% | 1% | 1.69% (1/59) |
| 40 jobs | LCP | 3% | 5% | 15.25% (9/59) |
| 40 jobs | TBT | 5% | 7.5% | 6.78% (4/59) |

Longer windows help detect smaller changes but do not reliably control false
alarms. The guarded adjustment still flags 15.9% of 20-job SSR TBT windows and
20.9% of its 40-job windows. A significant historical CPU slope is therefore not
sufficient to select a safe alarm policy. The t-statistic detector at threshold 7
has a different sensitivity/error trade-off; all variants are retained in the
[20-job](results/2026-10-18/history_w20_summary.csv) and
[40-job](results/2026-10-18/history_w40_summary.csv) tables.

The next CPD step is temporal hold-out calibration and evaluation of dependence,
not automatic use of the most sensitive detector as a merge gate.

## Remaining research boundaries

The [actual sitespeed comparison](sitespeed.md) completed nine jobs, with both
tools detecting the two target regressions in all three repetitions. The
[expanded open-source corpus](oss-cases.md) completed its first 16-job run.
These pilots are separate from the main A/A snapshot and need replication.
The [local comparison](local-comparison.md) adds three A/A jobs / 60 loads on
Apple M5 beside 109 matched CI jobs. Its short, single-machine sample supports
descriptive results, not a general ranking of local and hosted environments.

The corpus still covers one GitHub runner image and four demo pages. Injected
regressions mainly shift distributions; intermittent slow paths and changing tails
need a separate experiment. PELT, several changes in one window and drifts longer
than the observation window remain extensions. CPD is an evaluated research
prototype, not a production-ready alarm policy. The tool, article and thesis text
are later milestones in the roadmap.
